# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Casual gamers looking for an accessible, fun, and fast-paced chess variant.

## Product Purpose
Chaos Chess is a web-based chess variant featuring randomized special rules every 5 turns. It exists to provide an unpredictable, arcade-style experience that shakes up traditional chess mechanics. Success looks like an engaging, highly replayable game that is easy to pick up but wildly varied.

## Brand Personality
Playful, arcade, colorful, minimal, pixel-art.

## Anti-references
- No traditional wood or marble chessboards.
- No realistic 3D pieces. 
- Must stay strictly flat 2D pixel-art.

## Design Principles
- **Embrace the arcade:** Fast, responsive, and tactile.
- **Pixel perfection:** UI elements should align with the pixel-art aesthetic without compromising usability.
- **Clarity in chaos:** Even with randomized rules, the board state and active rules must remain instantly readable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Standard web contrast minimums.
- Colorblind-friendly piece and board distinction.
